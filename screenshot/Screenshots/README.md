📊 Retail Sales & Operations Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance, order status, customer reviews, delivery operations, and cancellation trends.

The dashboard transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights through interactive KPIs, charts, and operational metrics.

---

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze retail business performance and provide a clear view of:

- Overall sales and order performance
- Delivery and cancellation rates
- Order status distribution
- Customer review ratings
- Operational performance
- Product and category-level trends
- Business performance indicators

The dashboard is designed to help business stakeholders quickly identify operational issues and understand overall retail performance.

---

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI – Dashboard development & visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs
- Microsoft Excel – Source data
- Data Modeling – Relationships and analytical model

---

📌 Dashboard Features

1. KPI Overview

The dashboard provides high-level operational KPIs including:

KPI| Value
Cancelled Orders| 625
Delivered Orders| 96K
Delivery Rate| 97.02%
Cancellation Rate| 0.63%

These KPIs provide an immediate snapshot of overall order and delivery performance.

---

2. Order Status Analysis

A donut chart is used to visualize the distribution of orders across different statuses:

- Delivered
- Shipped
- Cancelled
- Unavailable
- Invoiced
- Processing
- Created
- Approved

This helps identify how orders are progressing through the operational pipeline.

---

3. Customer Review Analysis

A clustered bar chart analyzes customer review scores based on the count of review IDs.

This visualization helps understand customer satisfaction patterns and identify the distribution of ratings.

---

4. Operational Performance

The operational performance section provides a consolidated view of key business metrics to help monitor fulfillment and order-processing efficiency.
